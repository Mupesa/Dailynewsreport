- **The Mind-Expanding Fact (The "Tulipomania" Fallacy):** Most people cite the 17th-century Dutch Tulip craze as the ultimate example of market irrationality—where a single bulb cost as much as a house. However, economic historians (like Anne Goldgar) have debunked the myth that this destroyed the Dutch economy. The "folklore" persists because we *need* stories of moralizing market ruin to feel superior to the past.
